Questions

Good to know

Do we need to change our WhatsApp number?

We check your current WhatsApp setup and the integration options before we start. Whether a number migration or coexistence is needed depends on the provider and account.

Will Simplireply handle all patient conversations?

We manage the agreed automated and administrative follow-ups. Clinical questions, treatment advice and sensitive patient discussions stay with your clinic team.

Do we need a new CRM?

Not necessarily. We first look at the systems you already use, then set up the right way to track enquiries.

How long does setup take?

It depends on your enquiry sources, integrations and how complex the workflow is. You get a timeline after the audit.

Can you guarantee more appointments?

We improve how consistently you reply and follow up. Whether an enquiry becomes an appointment also depends on enquiry quality, clinic availability, pricing and your consultation process.

How much does it cost?

Managed services start from ₹7,500 per month. Setup and messaging charges are quoted separately.
